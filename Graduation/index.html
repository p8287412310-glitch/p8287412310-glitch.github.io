A Letter To My Sister

Dear Sis, Watching you graduate just feels so surreal, It feels like It was just yesterday we were all worried about where you'd end up looking for the results after CUET and crying after finding out you didn't get into college. But watching you go into the college surround yourself with a huge group of people have amazing talks just bewildered me and made me feel even more proud. You were worried whether you'll make friends or not and there were regrets regarding the college you got but, you just made the best out of the time you were given there, As, someone who has always watched you from the sidelines I knew it wouldn't be difficult for you to make friends or live your life to the fullest-Because, you're a ball of sunshine- But I am still proud of you for living your best college life with whatever you were given and graduating with such warmth and so many wins and feats I LOVE YOU and you will always be the biggest and sweetest inspiration I will ever have. SOMYA
